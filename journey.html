  The journey

Six steps between you and peace of mind.

A guided path through everything an Islamic will must contain, with evidence from Quran and Sunnah at every step.

01
Personal declaration
Your shahadah, your details, and the executors you trust, named in order of preference in case one is unavailable.
02
Religious obligations
Missed prayers, fasts, unpaid zakat, and pending Hajj. These are settled before any wealth is distributed.
03
Rights of people
Debts owed, amanah held, and anyone you may have wronged. You leave this world with a clean slate.
04
Inheritance distribution
The Fara'id calculator divides your estate automatically according to Shariah. Every heir's share carries its Quranic citation.
05
Funeral wishes
Who performs the ghusl, who leads the janazah, and where you are laid to rest. Clear instructions for your family.
06
Messages to loved ones
Your final words of love, advice, and dua for your spouse, children, parents, and family.
