Islam comes first. Your wasiyyah is a command from the Prophet ﷺ. It is an act of worship, valid before Allah the moment you complete it sincerely and have it properly witnessed. Nothing on this page changes that obligation.

This page repeats, in a permanent and always-accessible form, the same disclaimer shown before you begin your wasiyyah in the Tool.

Islamic guidance, not legal advice

Wasiyyah provides guidance based on the Quran and authentic Sunnah, upon the understanding of the Salaf al-Salih. It is not a substitute for legal advice from a qualified attorney in your country, and it is not a substitute for consulting a knowledgeable scholar about your specific circumstances.

Civil law may differ

We are Muslims living in a world whose legal systems were not built on our deen. Some countries have civil inheritance laws that may affect how an Islamic will is recognised or enforced. We acknowledge this reality. It does not change our religious obligation to write a wasiyyah, but it may affect how enforceable certain provisions are in a civil court. If you want your wasiyyah recognised legally in your country, have it reviewed by a local attorney after completing it here.

The inheritance calculator is an aid, not a final ruling

The Fara'id calculator is designed to apply established Islamic inheritance shares automatically, with citations for each ruling. For complex family situations, we recommend verifying the result with a qualified scholar before finalising your document.
