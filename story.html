  A letter from the founder

I built this because I was scared.

Not scared of death itself. As Muslims we know that death is a beginning, not an end. What scared me was the thought of leaving this world without my affairs in order, without my family knowing what I wanted.

One night, far from home, I asked myself what would happen if I didn't wake up. I had no answer. So I built one, slowly, with my own hands, asking Allah to accept it as an act of worship.

It is free, completely, with nothing held back, because the deen is not a product. It is private, because your wasiyyah is between you and Allah.

Built for the sake of Allah, for the ummah
